How this is maintained

  • Every figure comes from the vendor's own public pricing page, never from a third-party summary.
  • The whole table is re-checked on the same day each month, and the verification date at the top changes only when the check has actually happened.
  • When a price changes, the change is noted below rather than quietly overwritten.
  • No affiliate link appears on this page. Where a guide on this site does carry one, it is disclosed next to the link.
