How the recommendation is made

Each answer adds points to the platforms it genuinely suits, using the pricing and feature data on our pricing reference page, verified 19 August 2026. The platform with the most points wins; every point is shown to you at the end with the reason attached.

Three rules keep this honest. Affiliate relationships carry no weight in the scoring. Ties are broken against the platforms we earn from, never for them. And Substack, which has no affiliate programme at all, can win outright — it does, for people who answer that they want no fixed cost and no automation.

The nine questions

  1. How big is your list today?
  2. Where will the money come from?
  3. Do you need automated email sequences?
  4. What can you spend per month right now?
  5. Do you sell digital products?
  6. How much does email design matter to you?
  7. Do you want a public website and archive too?
  8. How big could the list get in twelve months?
  9. Are you moving an existing list?
