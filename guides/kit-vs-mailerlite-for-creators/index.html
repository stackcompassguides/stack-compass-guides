Kit vs MailerLite for creators starting a newsletter

Choose Kit if your newsletter is meant to become an audience-owned business. Choose MailerLite if you mainly need a lower-cost way to send polished email campaigns.

Best for MailerLite

Short recommendation

Start with Kit if your main goal is creator-led publishing, audience tagging, simple automations, and a path toward monetized newsletters or digital products. Consider MailerLite if your priority is keeping early email costs low and you mainly need straightforward campaigns and forms.

Choose Kit if...

  • You are launching a newsletter that may later sell products, sponsorships, courses, or memberships.
  • You want segments, tags, and automations without moving into enterprise marketing software.
  • You care about creator-specific workflows more than the absolute lowest starter price.

Choose MailerLite if...

  • You want the lowest-friction visual email editor and basic newsletter workflow.
  • You are optimizing early costs and do not need deep creator monetization features yet.
  • You prefer a simpler general-purpose email marketing suite.

Who should not choose Kit first

  • You only need a basic monthly newsletter and have no near-term monetization plan.
  • You are optimizing only for the lowest possible email platform cost.
  • You need advanced enterprise CRM functions rather than creator publishing workflows.
