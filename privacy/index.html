Privacy

Privacy-first by default.

This is a static site with no accounts, comments, payment forms or signup forms, no advertising scripts, and no cookies. The one measurement script we run is cookieless and collects no personal data.

What this site collects

This site sets no cookies and stores no personal data. It runs one privacy-first analytics script (Umami, EU-hosted, cookieless) which records aggregate page views, the referring site, and whether an affiliate link was clicked. It does not use cookies, does not fingerprint your device, and does not build a profile that follows you across sites. Nothing it records identifies you.

The site also stores one value in your browser’s session storage: which site sent you here, so that an affiliate link clicked later in the same visit is credited to the right source. It is deleted when you close the tab and never leaves your browser except as that source label.

The hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may process standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security and operations.

Affiliate links

Affiliate links carry tracking parameters so the affiliate network can attribute a referral. We add short identifiers to those links recording which site sent you, which position on the page the link sat in, and which page it was on — for example, a link in the hero section of the Kit vs MailerLite guide, clicked by a visitor who arrived from a search engine. They identify the link and the channel, not you.

Once you click through, the destination site and its affiliate network apply their own cookies and privacy policies, which are outside our control. Affiliate links are disclosed next to the link and on our affiliate disclosure page.

Email

If you email us, we use what you send to reply to you and to correct our pages. We do not add you to a mailing list.

Changes

Cookieless analytics were added on 19 August 2026. If this site ever adds cookies, a newsletter signup, advertising or any other data collection, this page will be updated before that happens.
