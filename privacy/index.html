Privacy

Privacy-first by default.

This is a static site with no accounts, comments, payment forms or signup forms — and no analytics or advertising scripts of any kind.

What this site collects

Nothing directly. There is no tracking script, no cookie set by this site, and no third-party embed on these pages. The hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may process standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security and operations.

Affiliate links

Affiliate links carry tracking parameters so the affiliate network can attribute a referral. We add a short identifier to those links that tells us which position on the page a click came from — for example the hero link versus the link at the end of the article. It identifies the link, not you.

Once you click through, the destination site and its affiliate network apply their own cookies and privacy policies, which are outside our control. Affiliate links are disclosed next to the link and on our affiliate disclosure page.

Email

If you email us, we use what you send to reply to you and to correct our pages. We do not add you to a mailing list.

Changes

If this site ever adds analytics, a newsletter or any other data collection, this page will be updated before that happens.
