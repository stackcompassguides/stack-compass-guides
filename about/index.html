About

Independent software comparisons for creators and small teams.

Stack Compass Guides exists to answer one kind of question well: given two or three credible tools, which one fits your situation, what does it cost, and what are you giving up by choosing it.

How a guide is built

  • Current official documentation. Feature claims come from the vendor's own product and help pages, read at the time of writing.
  • Pricing read from the source. Plans, limits and prices are taken from the live pricing pages, dated on the page, and linked so you can re-check them.
  • Feature comparison that uses numbers. Subscriber caps, send limits, plan tiers — not adjectives.
  • Clear trade-offs. Every guide states where the recommended tool is weaker than the alternative.
  • Fit and no-fit recommendations. We say who should choose a tool and who should not, rather than crowning a universal winner.

What you will not find here

  • Claims of hands-on testing we have not done, or invented user experiences.
  • Scores and badges that imply lab work behind them.
  • Recommendations shaped by commission rates. Affiliate relationships are disclosed next to the link and do not change the guidance.

Corrections

Prices and plan limits change often. If something on a guide is out of date or wrong, tell us at our contact address and we will correct or re-date the page.
