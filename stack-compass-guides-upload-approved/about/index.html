About

Research-led software comparisons without pretending to be a large publication.

Stack Compass Guides helps creators, freelancers, and small teams compare digital tools for practical work decisions.

Editorial Approach

Our content is based on research, official sources, product documentation, pricing pages, and clearly documented feature information. If we have not directly evaluated a product, we will not claim that we have.

Some pages may include affiliate links. Commercial relationships do not change the price paid by the reader and should not change the fit/no-fit guidance on the page.

What We Avoid

  • Fake reviews or invented user experiences.
  • Scored badges that imply evaluation work we have not done.
  • Claims of a large editorial team or years of experience.
  • Recommendations that ignore who should not buy the product.
