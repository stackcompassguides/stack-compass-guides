Affiliate Disclosure

Some links on this site earn us a commission.

If you sign up or buy through an affiliate link on Stack Compass Guides, we may receive a commission from the vendor or its affiliate network. It never costs you more, and it does not change the price or terms you are offered.

Which links these are

Affiliate links are marked with a disclosure directly next to them, and are tagged rel="sponsored" in the page source. Links in the Sources list at the foot of a guide, and links used to cite a price or a plan limit, are always plain editorial links with no commercial relationship — even where we do have an affiliate relationship with that vendor. If a link earns us money, it says so beside it.

How we keep it honest

  • A commission never buys a recommendation. Where a competing tool is the better fit, we say so and link to it without earning anything.
  • We publish the downsides of the products we recommend, and who should not buy them.
  • An affiliate relationship is not product experience. Where we have not used a product in production, we do not claim to have.
  • Prices and plan limits are sourced from the vendor's own pages and dated. Always verify current pricing and terms with the vendor before you buy.
